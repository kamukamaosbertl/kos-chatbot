# About Kamukama Osbert

## Who he is
Kamukama Osbert (initials KOS) is a final-year (year four) Software Engineering student at Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST) in Mbarara, Uganda. He builds web and mobile applications, develops AI solutions with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and prompt engineering, and works in cyber security. He is also a graphic designer and the Founder & CEO of Virtual Room Beddings & Comfort, a linen business in Mbarara.

## Strongest skills and tech stack
His strongest stack is JavaScript with React for fast, interactive user interfaces, and Python with FastAPI for secure, high-performance APIs and AI backends. He also builds cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter.
Other skills: RAG systems, prompt engineering, ethical hacking, digital forensics, Bash scripting on Kali Linux, web and mobile app development, and graphic design.
